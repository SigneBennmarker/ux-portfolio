The Challenge

SolidSport’s portal was powerful but overwhelming. Channel administrators struggled to find and understand their livestream statistics, which led to frequent support tickets and frustration. The company needed a fresh design that would make statistics easy to access, engaging, and useful—while fitting into their upcoming brand relaunch.

My Impact

led the full UX design process—from competitor analysis and data visualization exploration to prototyping and usability testing. The result was a new statistics dashboard that: - Reduced complex navigation steps by 40% through clearer information hierarchy. - Increased understandability in user tests by 75%, with participants completing key tasks faster and with fewer errors.

Designing for Motivation, Not Just Metrics

Instead of simply showing numbers, I focused on motivating channel admins to keep streaming. For example: - Growth-focused graphs (e.g., “New paying viewers”) highlighted progress over time. - Clear language and tooltips helped non-experts interpret data. - Export options (CSV & PDF) ensured both casual and advanced users could share insights easily.

From Research to Prototype

- Competitor analysis: Studied YouTube, Twitch, and Instagram to identify best practices in video statistics. - Visualization strategy: Prioritized simple, engaging charts to avoid overwhelming non-technical users. - Prototyping in Figma: Balanced SolidSport’s bold red brand with new semantic color indicators for clarity (e.g., green for growth). - User testing: Ran A/B tests with SolidSport employees simulating real admins, revealing that the simplified dashboard cut task completion time by nearly half.

Outcome

The final Figma prototype offered a mobile- and desktop-ready statistics dashboard that integrated seamlessly with SolidSport’s relaunch vision. It not only reduced reliance on customer support but also encouraged community engagement by making data actionable and motivating. The work was handed off with structured design files and developer-ready documentation, ensuring smooth implementation.
